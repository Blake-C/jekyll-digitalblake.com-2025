description: A personalization layer for WordPress block themes. It works out a visitor's market segment and renders the matching layout, so a SaaS company can show one message to finance and another to healthcare. Segment detection uses the visitor's IP address today, and in production it would more likely come from a ZoomInfo or 6sense signal. This is not production ready, but more of an experiment of how I would implement it inside of WordPress.

The Southwest National Primate Research Center (SNPRC) is the sister site to the Texas Biomedical Research Institute, and the two organizations share many of the same scientists. The client didn't want to maintain duplicate scientist bios across two WordPress installs, one source of truth, two presentations.

I built SNPRC's scientist pages to consume the WordPress REST API from Texas BioMed directly, so a scientist's bio, credentials, and publications are entered once on the parent site and rendered on SNPRC automatically, always in sync with no manual process. For the visual layer I built the site around SVG icons and logos so the brand reads sharp at any resolution, from a phone up to a 5K display, and carried SNPRC's own identity across a design system that shares its DNA with Texas BioMed without feeling like a copy of it.

**Impact:** Page load times reduced from 3s to under 2s. The scientist directory is fully filterable with smooth animations, making it easy for research partners to find and navigate the team.
